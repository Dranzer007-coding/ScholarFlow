# Problem Statement

## Background

The scholarship approval process in many organizations is still largely manual. Students submit applications along with supporting documents, and officers verify every document one by one before approving or rejecting the application.

This process is time-consuming, repetitive, and prone to human errors.

---

## Existing Challenges

- Manual verification of uploaded documents.
- Long processing and approval time.
- High workload for scholarship officers.
- Difficulty in detecting fake or duplicate documents.
- Lack of transparency during the approval process.
- Increased chances of human error.

---

## Proposed Solution

ScholarFlow AI is an AI-powered scholarship approval platform that automates document verification, eligibility checking, fraud detection, and officer assistance.

Instead of replacing human officers, the system provides AI-generated recommendations while allowing officers to make the final decision.

The platform aims to make scholarship approval faster, more accurate, transparent, and secure.
