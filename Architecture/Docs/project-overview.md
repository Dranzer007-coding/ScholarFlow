# Project Overview

## Introduction

ScholarFlow AI is an AI-powered scholarship approval and verification platform designed to simplify and automate the scholarship management process.

The platform allows students to submit scholarship applications online along with the required documents. Multiple AI agents then assist in verifying documents, extracting information, checking eligibility, detecting fraud, and supporting officers in making informed approval decisions.

The final approval or rejection always remains with the authorized officer, ensuring human oversight and transparency.

---

## Objectives

The main objectives of ScholarFlow AI are:

- Reduce manual verification work.
- Speed up scholarship approval.
- Improve accuracy using AI.
- Detect fake or duplicate documents.
- Maintain transparency through audit logs.
- Assist officers with AI-generated recommendations.
- Provide a secure and user-friendly platform for students and officers.

---

## Target Users

### Students
- Register and log in.
- Apply for scholarships.
- Upload required documents.
- Track application status.

### Scholarship Officers
- Review AI-generated verification reports.
- Approve or reject applications.
- Request document corrections.
- Monitor application progress.

### Administrators
- Manage users.
- Configure scholarship schemes.
- Monitor system activities.
- View audit logs.

---

## Expected Outcome

ScholarFlow AI aims to make scholarship approval faster, more transparent, secure, and reliable while reducing the workload of scholarship officers through intelligent AI assistance.
